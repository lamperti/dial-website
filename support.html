Dial · Espresso dial-in for iPhone

If something feels off, or your grinder isn't in the list, tap Feedback on Dial's home screen. It comes straight to me, and I read every message. You can also write to hello@dialespresso.app.

Getting started

  • Pick your grinder at the top of the home screen. More than 40 come with their real scale and steps, or add your own.
  • Add a bag by picking the roaster and the coffee. Dial fills in the rest, and you set the roast date once.
  • Log a shot with its grind, its time and how it tasted. Dial suggests where to go next and tells you why.

Common questions

Why does Dial suggest such a small change?
Because it measures how many seconds one step on your grinder is worth, from your own shots. Early on it starts from a careful guess, and the guess gets closer the more you pull.

A shot ran far from the others at the same grind.
That happens, usually because of puck prep or the dose. When one shot lands far from the rest, Dial says it's likely the puck, not the grind, and keeps the setting.

Will I lose my shots on a new phone?
Not if you're signed in to iCloud. Dial syncs through your private iCloud database, and your shelf comes back on the new phone.

Which scales work?
Dial reads the weight and timer of an Acaia Lunar over Bluetooth. Without a scale, type the time and the yield yourself.

Privacy

Dial has no account and no ads, and it doesn't track you. The details are in the privacy policy.
