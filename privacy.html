Privacy Policy

Dial · Last updated November 23, 2026

I'm Christophe Lamperti, and I make Dial on my own. This page explains what the app collects and why. In short, there's no account, your coffee data stays on your devices, and the little that leaves them is either anonymous or something you chose to send.

Your coffee data

Your grinders, bags, shots, tastes and ratings are stored on your iPhone. There is no Dial account and no Dial server holding them.

If you are signed in to iCloud, Dial keeps this data in sync through your own private iCloud database (Apple's CloudKit), so a new iPhone gets your shelf back. That database belongs to your Apple account, and I can't see or read it. You can turn it off in iOS Settings, under your name, iCloud, and delete it from there.

Your scale

If you connect a Bluetooth scale, Dial reads its weight and timer during a shot. These readings are used on the phone and saved with the shot, nowhere else.

Anonymous usage counts

Dial uses TelemetryDeck (telemetrydeck.com), an analytics service based in the EU, to count things like app opens, bags added and shots logged, and a few bounded details such as where the taste slider sat when a shot was saved. These signals carry a hashed, anonymous device identifier, never your name, email, location or advertising identifier, and never text you typed. They are used only to understand how the app is used and what to improve, never for advertising or to track you across apps.

Feedback you choose to send

The Feedback button lets you write to me. If you send a message, Dial transmits:

  • the message you typed,
  • your email address, only if you enter one,
  • the app version, your device model and iOS version, to help reproduce problems.

This goes to Basin (usebasin.com), a form service, which forwards it to me by email. Your email is used to reply to you. If you also check "Also send me short updates as the app grows", it may be used to send you occasional news about Dial, and you can ask to stop at any time.

What Dial does not do

  • No ads, no advertising identifier (IDFA), no tracking.
  • No sale or sharing of your data with data brokers.
  • No location, contacts, photos or health data.

Your choices

To have your feedback messages or email address deleted, or to stop receiving updates, write to hello@dialespresso.app. To delete your coffee data, remove the app, and if sync was on, delete Dial's data from iCloud in iOS Settings.

Children

Dial is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
